# Stewardship of Information: Curated Readings for Statistics and Data Science Courses

**Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald** | Version 1.0 | October 2026

## About These Materials

These reading lists accompany the manuscript by Kaitlyn G. Fitzgerald titled "Becoming Good Stewards of Information: A Framework for Integrating Ethical, Civic, and Professional Formation Throughout the Statistics and Data Science Curriculum," available at (https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23352)[https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23352]. The article presents stewardship of information as a framework organized around a foundational premise (statistics and data science are human endeavors) and four dimensions: statistical literacy and evidence-based decision-making, statistical and data communication, data ethics and responsible use of data, and data for social good and civic statistics. The lists below are the weekly readings used to implement the framework in Introduction to Statistics, Probability, Data Science, and upper-division statistics courses. Full citations and a discussion of how each reading is used appear in the article.

In my courses, these readings are assigned on [Perusall](https://www.perusall.com), a free collaborative annotation platform, in about 10 weeks of a 15-week semester. Although I refer to them simply as "readings," some are videos or podcasts. The readings are meant to be illustrative rather than prescriptive. Instructors are encouraged to adapt them to their own course content, audience, and current events.

**Access note:** Some readings are from news outlets or magazines that may be paywalled. Many are available through university library subscriptions, and instructors should consult their library about options for course use.

## Framework Dimension Labels

The last column of each table uses shorthand for the framework's four dimensions:

- **Literacy**: statistical literacy and evidence-based decision-making
- **Communication**: statistical and data communication
- **Ethics**: data ethics and responsible use of data
- **Social good**: data for social good and civic statistics

***Human endeavors*** (italicized) marks readings that explicitly foreground the framework's foundational premise that statistics and data science are human endeavors.
